Who's on stage

Josh Mannas brings raw, unfiltered laughs built from real events and real people — pushed and reshaped just enough to land the joke. His range runs wide: unapologetic bits about relationships — dating, married, all of it — dad jokes earned from raising two kids, stories that poke fun at himself just as much as anyone else, and his own unmistakable take on everyday chaos (Costco runs included).

On stage, he brings magnetic, high-energy delivery — animated, physical, and expressive, the kind of presence bookers and audiences keep pointing to. He mostly steers clear of political material, though a joke built purely for the laugh can occasionally come across as taking a side — that's the bit talking, not his own opinion.
